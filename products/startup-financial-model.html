Description

Show investors a model that ties out, not a page of guessed numbers. You enter customers, pricing, churn, headcount and costs in one place. The workbook builds all three statements from those drivers. The Balance Sheet balances in every year because nothing is plugged.

Assumptions tab: every input lives here, grouped by revenue, headcount, opex, capex, working capital and financing.

Monthly tab: a 36-month build from revenue down to ending cash. A flag warns you when cash runs low.

Annual tab: a 5-year Income Statement, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Statement. A check row shows the Balance Sheet ties to 0.00 each year.

Unit Economics tab: CAC, LTV, LTV to CAC and payback period by year. CAC here counts the marketing budget only.

Runway and Raise tab: months of runway, the cash-out month and raise sizing. A 5x5 grid shows Year 3 revenue across growth and churn.

Charts: revenue by stream, ending cash and headcount, ready for a deck.

Every output is an open formula that you can click and trace. Blue cells are inputs, black cells are formulas and green cells link to other tabs. Nothing is locked and there are no macros. Built with AI assistance and formula-checked.
