Description

You have SAFEs outstanding, a term sheet on the way and no clear idea how much of the company you will own after the round. This Excel cap table answers that before you sign. It is built for the messy middle of a raise, not just the clean table at the end.

Current Cap Table: enter founders, employees, advisors and the option pool. Fully diluted totals feed every other tab.

SAFEs and Notes: enter each instrument's amount, type, cap and discount, plus interest and term for notes. Each one converts at the better of its cap price and its discount price.

Priced Round: enter the pre-money valuation and new investors. The tab solves the price per share and sizes the option pool top-up, before or after the new money. Post-money SAFEs follow the YC document, so the holder gets Amount divided by Cap of the company and the extra dilution lands on the founders. That step is solved in closed form, so there is no circular reference.

Dilution Scenarios: three raise scenarios side by side, a hypothetical Series A on top of each and a chart of founder ownership.

Exit Waterfall: a 1x non-participating liquidation preference with a participating toggle, at three exit values, charted by holder class.

It opens with a sample two-founder seed round loaded so you can watch every formula work first. The founders hold 4,500,000 and 3,500,000 shares next to a 1,000,000-share pool. There are two SAFEs. One is a $500,000 post-money SAFE at an $8,000,000 cap. The other is a $250,000 pre-money SAFE at a $6,000,000 cap with a 20 percent discount. The round is $3,000,000 at a $12,000,000 pre-money with a 10 percent post-money pool. Replace those numbers with your own.

Every non-input cell is a live Excel formula. Nothing is hidden or locked, so you can trace any number to its source. Built with AI assistance and formula-checked. Built for Excel. Instant digital download, no physical item ships.
