Description

Stop guessing whether a rental deal actually works. This Excel workbook takes a property's price, financing, rent, and expenses and turns them into the numbers that matter: monthly cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, DSCR, the 1% and 50% rules, and a plain PASS, CAUTION, or FAIL scorecard you can see at a glance.

Type your numbers into the blue cells on the Deal tab and everything else updates instantly, including a full 10-year projection with rent growth, expense growth, appreciation, loan paydown, a sale in your chosen hold year, IRR, and equity multiple. A complete month-by-month amortization schedule runs the full loan term. A Compare tab lets you line up to four properties side by side, with the best cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash, and DSCR highlighted automatically. Two built-in charts show yearly cash flow and equity versus loan balance so the trend is obvious without reading a single number.

This is built for small landlords and house hackers, not institutional analysts. There is no macro, no plugin, and nothing to install. It opens pre-filled with a sample duplex deal so you can see exactly how it works before you touch a single cell, then clear it out and run your own numbers. The scorecard's thresholds live in their own editable block, so if your bar for a good deal is different from the defaults, you can change it in ten seconds.

Every non-input number in this workbook is a live formula: PMT for the mortgage payment, CUMPRINC for principal paydown, IRR for the 10-year return. Built with AI assistance and formula-checked; formulas are fully open, so you can click any cell and see exactly how the answer was built, learn from it, or adapt it.
